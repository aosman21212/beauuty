Beauty Spa Management

Complete salon & spa management for Odoo 19 — appointments, therapists, packages & invoicing.

🌸 Calendar View 💆 Therapist Profiles 🧴 Treatment Packages 📋 Kanban Board 🧾 One-Click Invoice 📄 PDF Confirmation 🔒 Role-Based Access 🎭 Demo Data

Key Features

📅

Appointment Calendar

Drag-and-drop weekly/monthly calendar. Colour-coded by therapist. Overlap detection prevents double-booking.

💆

Therapist Management

Profiles with photo, specialisation, contact info and service assignments. Smart button shows appointment history.

🧴

Services & Categories

Full service catalogue with categories, duration, pricing and optional linkage to invoiceable Odoo products.

🎁

Treatment Packages

Bundle multiple services at a special price. Savings calculated automatically. Apply a package to any appointment.

🏠

Treatment Rooms

Manage room capacity and notes. Assign a room per appointment to avoid conflicts.

🧾

Invoice Integration

Generate an Odoo invoice directly from a confirmed appointment with one click. Payment status shown on the form.

⊞

Kanban by Status

Visual pipeline: New → Confirmed → In Progress → Done. Drag cards between columns or use status buttons.

📄

PDF Confirmation

Print a professional appointment confirmation for clients, including service list, therapist and room details.

🔒

Role-Based Security

Two built-in groups: Manager (full access) and Receptionist (read & book, no deletion).
